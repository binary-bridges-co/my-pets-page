# Help

My Pets keeps your pet's health record, reminders and documents on your iPhone and in your iCloud. Here are answers to the questions people ask most. If yours isn't here, write to us at [support@binarybridges.co](mailto:support@binarybridges.co).

## Getting started

**Do I need an account?** No. My Pets has no accounts. It uses your device's iCloud account for backup and sharing, and works without one.

**How do I add a pet?** Tap + on the Pets screen. A name and a species are enough; you can add the rest later.

**Can I change something I recorded?** Every pet detail can be updated, and every record can be corrected or voided. The change is a new record, so the history keeps what was there before.

## Reminders and alarms

**I don't get dose reminders.** Check Settings › Notifications › My Pets is allowed. For a dose that must not be missed, turn on its alarm: alarms ring even when your iPhone is on silent.

**Can I mark a dose without opening the app?** Yes: from the notification, the widget, the Lock Screen, or with Siri.

## iCloud and a new iPhone

**Are my records backed up?** Yes, in your private iCloud, when iCloud is on for My Pets. Only your devices can read them.

**I have a new iPhone.** Sign in with the same iCloud account and open My Pets: your pets come back. If you lost access to your old devices, use the recovery code you saved in Settings.

**What is the recovery code for?** Your records are signed with a key that lives on your devices. The recovery code brings that key back if you lose every device or change iCloud accounts, so you can keep adding to your pets' records. Keep it somewhere safe and offline.

## Sharing

**How do I share a pet with my family?** Open the pet's sharing options and invite people through iCloud. They see the record read-only; only you add to it.

**Someone near me doesn't use iCloud.** Pass them a copy in person, phone to phone, or export a file.

**I'm giving my pet to a new owner.** Use Transfer: the new owner gets the complete history and becomes the one who adds to it.

## Collar tag and lost mode

**Which tags work?** NFC tags of type NTAG213, NTAG215 or NTAG216. NTAG213 holds the link only; the larger ones also hold the card, so it can be read without signal. You can also print the tag's QR code or send it to an engraver.

**What does a finder see?** What you chose to show: for example your phone, your pet's name and photo, and, while it's lost, an approximate area and your message. Never your address or the exact spot. The finder needs no app.

**How do I stop showing the page?** Turn the public page off for the pet, or deactivate the tag. Deactivating a tag is final; make a new link for a new tag.

## Documents

**Can I give my vet a copy?** Export the carnet PDF from the pet's Health card. Its code lets another My Pets user check it against the signed record.

**Can I keep everything outside the app?** Export all your data from Settings to a file. Any copy of My Pets can import and verify it.
