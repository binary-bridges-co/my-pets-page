# Yearly prices of pet health apps with subscriptions

The source of the homepage's comparison line, "Pet health apps with subscriptions charge about US$20-50 a year." (`site/src/content/en.ts`, `PRICE.comparison`). This repository is public, so the apps are not named here.

- **Checked:** 19 November 2026.
- **Method:** a web search of US App Store listings (`apps.apple.com/us`) of pet health record apps for iPhone, then each listing's "In-App Purchases" section read on its US page. Only auto-renewing yearly tiers count; monthly and lifetime purchases are left out.
- **Seen (US dollars a year):**

| App | Yearly tiers |
|---|---|
| A | 19.99 |
| B | 19.99 |
| C | 24.99 |
| D | 23.99 (starter), 47.99 (pro), 99.99 (breeder tier) |
| E | 9.49, 19.00, 28.90, 47.90 (by family size) |

- **Reading:** 8 of the 10 yearly tiers fall between US$19.00 and US$47.99. The two outside are a single-pet tier at US$9.49 and a breeder tier at US$99.99. Every app's entry or main yearly tier is US$19.00-24.99, and the larger tiers reach about US$48. "About US$20-50 a year" holds for the typical tiers, so the figure is kept.
- **Re-check:** before each pricing or launch change, and at least once a year. If the typical tiers move out of the range, use the fallback wording from the plan: "Many pet apps charge every year. My Pets is one payment."
